Partners

Initiatives and tools that EEG101 works with and supports

EEG101 works alongside a growing network of partner organisations and initiatives that share our commitment to open, rigorous, and globally inclusive EEG science.

Partner initiatives

#EEGManyLabs

Large-scale multi-site replication of 20 influential EEG studies across >230 laboratories from 32 countries.

Visit website ↗

CuttingEEG

Community association promoting cutting-edge EEG methods through the CuttingGardens distributed conference model.

Visit website ↗

INDOS

INDoS is a European COST Action focused on transforming how human neuroimaging data is shared and reused.

Visit website ↗

Global Brain Consortium

International network promoting diversity and inclusion in neuroscience research across the globe.

Visit website ↗

Global Brain Health Institute

International organisation dedicated to protecting the world’s ageing populations from threats to brain health.

Visit website ↗

African Brain Data Network

Network promoting open, FAIR neuroscience data from African research sites to advance globally representative brain science.

Visit website ↗

Partner tools & platforms

FieldTrip

Open-source MATLAB toolbox for advanced analysis of MEG, EEG, and invasive electrophysiological data.

Visit website ↗

ARTEM-IS

INCF-developed standardised reporting tools for EEG research, providing web-based guidance forms for experimental documentation.

Visit website ↗

EEGNet

EEGNet provides a centralized platform to combine standardized annotated EEG data and analytics for global health applications, by harmonizing data formats and computational tools.

Visit website ↗
