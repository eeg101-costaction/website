## Analytics

This website uses [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com) (GA4) to collect anonymised data about how visitors use the site. This includes information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, approximate geographic location (country level), device type, and how you arrived at the site (for example, via a search engine or a direct link).

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish between visits. No personally identifiable information is collected or stored by EEG101. The data is processed by Google LLC and may be transferred to servers outside the UK and European Economic Area. For details of how Google handles this data, please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Contact data

When you contact EEG101 by email, your email address and message are used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not share contact data with third parties.

## Event registration

When you register for an EEG101 meeting, training session, workshop, or Working Group activity through a booking action on the Events page or calendar, we collect your first and last name, email address, institution, country, whether you are under 40 (Young Researcher and Innovator status), whether you are a member of the EEG101 COST Action, and your gender, for which "Prefer not to say" is always an option. This information is used to administer the relevant event, manage capacity and waiting lists, send registration updates and calendar invitations, maintain an attendance record, and report anonymised participation figures, such as gender balance, Young Researcher and Innovator participation and EEG101 membership, as part of COST Action reporting. Because EEG101 events may be recorded, registration also asks you to consent to being recorded and to the recording being published online, for example in the EEG101 Library and on the EEG101 YouTube channel.

Registration information is held in a private EEG101 registration spreadsheet and can be accessed only by authorised members of the organising team. The public website does not display attendee information. The registration form records your consent to this use of your information. Records are ordinarily deleted within 12 months of the completion of this COST Action, subject to any documented administrative, financial, or legal retention requirement.

The event-booking service uses a private Google Apps Script to transfer form submissions to the registration spreadsheet and send confirmation messages. You can contact [eeg101costaction@gmail.com](mailto:eeg101costaction@gmail.com) to ask about your registration information or request its correction or deletion, subject to the limits described above.

## Grant applications

When you apply for an EEG101 grant through the application form on the Grants page, we collect your name, email address, position, career stage, institution and its country (and so whether it is in an Inclusiveness Target Country), your ORCID iD if you give it, whether you are under 40, whether you are an early-career researcher, your gender, for which "Prefer not to say" is always an option, your EEG101 membership and Working Groups, the details of your proposed activity and budget, and the documents you upload, such as your CV and letters of support. This information is used to assess your application, to decide on and administer funding, to contact you about your application, and to report anonymised totals, such as the amounts awarded by scheme, gender, country, ITC and Young Researcher and Innovator status, as part of COST Action reporting.

Applications and their documents are held in a private EEG101 spreadsheet and Google Drive folder. They can be accessed only by the EEG101 grants team and the grant reviewers appointed for the call, who are asked to declare any conflict of interest. The public website does not display applications. A confirmation of each application is emailed to the applicant and copied to the EEG101 grants team. Applications are ordinarily deleted within 12 months of the completion of this COST Action, subject to any documented administrative, financial, or legal retention requirement, for example where COST requires grant records to be kept. You can contact [eeg101costaction@gmail.com](mailto:eeg101costaction@gmail.com) to ask about your application data or request its correction.

## COST platform (e-COST)

Membership of EEG101 is managed through the [e-COST platform](https://e-services.cost.eu/action/CA24148/working-groups/apply), operated by COST. Please refer to [COST's privacy policy](https://www.cost.eu/privacy-policy/) for details of how your data is handled in that context.
