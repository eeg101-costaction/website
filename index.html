About EEG101

Electroencephalography (EEG) is the most widely used functional brain imaging modality worldwide, yet the field is held back by substantial fragmentation — in hardware, protocols, analytical workflows, and access to training.

EEG101 is a COST Action network launched in 2025, exactly 101 years after Hans Berger's discovery of EEG. Bringing together researchers from across Europe and beyond, EEG101 addresses the fragmentation that limits collaboration, data sharing, and reproducibility in EEG research.

Our mission is to establish a coordinated, open, and collaborative network that harmonises methodologies, improves data accessibility, and provides equitable training opportunities — establishing Europe as a global leader in open EEG science.

Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

EEG101 is fully committed to the COST inclusiveness policy centred around geographical spread, researchers from all career stages, and gender balance. Fostering fair and diverse scientific communities, EEG101 encourages its members to embrace the ALBA declaration promoting equity and inclusivity on individual and workplace levels.

What is a COST Action?

COST (European Cooperation in Science and Technology) is a funding agency for research and innovation networks. COST Actions help connect research initiatives across Europe and enable scientists to grow their ideas by sharing them with their peers. This boosts their research, career and innovation.

Working Group 3

The EEG Community Framework

A deontological framework for EEG science: the standards we believe are essential to collectively maximise the positive impact of EEG research for society.

Written by 43 contributors from across the community, the Framework sets out commitments across three domains. You can sign all of it, or only the parts you support — every commitment is recorded separately, so signing is a statement of what you actually agree with rather than a single yes or no.

  • 1 Validity and scientific integrity Reproducible practice, standardised and documented data, open science and open source, and theory-driven, interpretable modelling.
  • 2 Democratization Diversity in the scientific community and in study populations, and accessibility of research outcomes.
  • 3 Responsibility Ethical use of EEG, realistic claims about technology, research evaluation, and the environmental footprint of our work.

Our approach

EEG101 works to advance open and rigorous EEG science across Europe through the following mechanisms and approaches.

Standardise

Develop and disseminate standardised reporting guidelines, pre-registration templates, and analytical workflows to promote consistency and reproducibility across European EEG laboratories.

Integrate

Build a centralised platform hosting a meta-directory of FAIR-compliant datasets, standardised pipelines, and a database of open-source software tools — making EEG data accessible and interoperable.

Advance

Drive innovation through Short-Term Scientific Missions, Virtual Mobility Grants, and a distributed conference model — building capacity and connecting the next generation of EEG researchers.

Collaborate

Connect researchers across disciplines, countries, and sectors. EEG101 builds on the extensive networks of existing initiatives engaging over 3,000 scientists worldwide.

Capacity-build

Prioritise Young Researchers & Innovators and researchers from Inclusiveness Target Countries through dedicated funding, mentorship, and open training materials.
