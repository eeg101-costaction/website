Why this mattered

Aligning coordination with technical collaboration

The mission brought together the EEG101 Chair and Vice-Chair to align Grant Period 1 priorities, prepare the framework for Grant Period 2, and strengthen coordination across the Action. It also supported Working Group 2 activity by connecting discussions of EEG data curation and harmonisation with Research Software Engineering perspectives on scientific software, interoperability, and data standardisation.
