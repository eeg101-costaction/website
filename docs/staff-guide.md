# Updating the EEG101 website: a guide for staff

You can add and change **Events**, **other calendar dates** (deadlines, calls) and
**Spotlights** yourself, through a simple online editor. No technical knowledge
or special account is needed: just the email address an admin invited.

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**.
2. Type your email address and press **Continue**. (Do not choose "Sign in with
   GitHub" — that is for admins.)
3. You will get an email titled **"Your Pages CMS temporary code"**. Type the
   6-digit code into the page. The code lasts 5 minutes; if it has expired,
   just ask for another. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder.
4. Open **eeg101-costaction/website**.

You will see **Events**, **Other calendar dates** and **Spotlights** on the left.

## The one thing to know: drafts, review, then publish

Every new item starts as a **draft**. A draft is not on the website: you can
save it as often as you like, over as many sessions as you need, and come back
to it later from the list.

1. **Write it.** Add the item and press **Save** whenever you want to keep your
   work. Leave **Published on the website** unticked.
2. **Look at it.** A couple of minutes after each save, the draft appears on the
   drafts page, **www.eeg101.eu/drafts/**, exactly as it will look on the site.
   (That page is not linked from anywhere, but it is not secret either: see
   below.)
3. **Ask for review.** When it is finished, tick **Ready for review** and save.
   Marie-Constance and Annalisa get an email asking them to review it.
4. **Publish.** The reviewer reads it on the drafts page. If it is ready, they
   open it in the editor, tick **Published on the website** and save: it goes
   live a minute or two later. If it needs changes, they untick **Ready for
   review**, save, and tell you what to change.

Once an item is published, changes to it go live as soon as you save. To take
something off the website again, untick **Published on the website** and save:
it becomes a draft again.

**Review is a team rule, not a lock.** The editor cannot stop someone from
ticking Published themselves, so please do not publish your own items without a
review (correcting a typo in something already live is fine).

**Drafts are not confidential.** Nobody will find the drafts page by accident,
but the website's files are public on GitHub, drafts included. Do not put
embargoed or confidential information in a draft; write that in a private
document until it can be public.

If you make a mistake, just edit the item again and save. Nothing you do can
break the website for visitors.

## Adding an event

1. Click **Events**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the form. Each field has a short explanation underneath. The ones
   you will always need: **Title**, **What kind of item is this?**, **Date**,
   **Format**, **Short description**, and ideally a **Picture** with its
   **Picture description**.
3. If it has a time, also choose **Time zone of those times**. The Calendar uses
   it to show each visitor the time in their own zone.
4. Press **Save**. It is saved as a draft (see above).

Once published, the event appears on the **Events** page and the **Calendar**.

**Events run by another organisation** (a conference we are attending): tick
**Run by another organisation**. They are listed under "External events".

**Taking bookings on the website:** tick **Take bookings on this website**, set
**Bookings are** to **Open**, and fill in the start time, end time and time zone.
A Register button appears. To stop taking bookings, set it to **Closed**.

**Members-only events** (COST members, invited through e-COST): choose
"EEG101 COST members only" under **Who can attend?**, and leave bookings off and
the joining link empty.

> **Careful with joining links.** Anything typed in **Online joining link** is
> public, and is emailed automatically to everyone who has booked. If the link
> must stay private, leave it empty and ask an admin to add it to the
> registration workbook instead.

## Adding a deadline or other date

Use **Other calendar dates** for things that are not events: grant deadlines,
calls, holidays. They appear on the **Calendar** only. Give it a **Title**, a
**Kind** and a **Date**, and optionally a time and a link (for example to
`/grants/`).

**Everything here is public.** Internal meetings go in the internal Google
Calendar, not here.

## Adding a Spotlight

1. Click **Spotlights**, then **Add an entry**.
2. The form follows the page from top to bottom:
   - the title and the main picture;
   - a summary, and when and where it happened;
   - three short sections: **why it mattered**, **what it enabled** and
     **what comes next**, each with an optional heading and some text;
   - lists of key outcomes, people and places, and next steps;
   - the buttons at the end.
3. **Finish date** puts the Spotlights in order, newest first.
4. Tick **Show on the home page** to feature it. The most recent featured
   Spotlight is the one shown there.
5. Press **Save**. Once published, the page appears at
   `www.eeg101.eu/spotlights/...`.

It is easiest to open an existing Spotlight to see how one is filled in.

## Pictures

- Click the picture field and **Upload**. JPG, PNG and WebP pictures are
  accepted. Large phone photos are shrunk automatically a minute after you
  save, turned the right way up, and stripped of hidden location data.
- **Before uploading a photo taken on a phone, remove its location** (on an
  iPhone: Share → Options → turn off Location; on Android: in the photo's
  details, remove the location), or turn off location in the camera settings.
  The automatic clean-up only fixes the copy on the website; the original you
  uploaded stays in the site's public history.
- Landscape (wide) pictures work best for events and Spotlights.
- Always fill in **Picture description**: one sentence describing the picture,
  for people who cannot see it.

## Reviewing a draft (for reviewers)

You get an email from GitHub titled **"Ready for review: ..."** with two links:

- **the drafts page**, where the draft is shown as it will look on the site;
- **the editor**, to open the item.

Read it on the drafts page (check names, dates, times, links and the picture).
Then open it in the editor and either tick **Published on the website** and
save, or untick **Ready for review**, save, and reply to the email with what
needs changing. The request closes by itself once the item is published.

## Things you cannot change here

- The **web address** (filename) of an event or Spotlight is fixed once it is
  created, so shared links and bookings keep working. If you got the title
  badly wrong, ask an admin.
- Other pages (Team, Library, Video Hub...) are still edited by an admin.

## If something goes wrong

Your change not appearing after 10 minutes, or something looking wrong on the
site? Tell the website admin. After every save, an automatic check looks for
problems and alerts the admins, so they may already know.
