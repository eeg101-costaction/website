OPES is led by WG2 co-leader Prof Dr Claudio Babiloni in collaboration with colleagues across the EEG101 network. It combines expert input with resting-state EEG datasets from different laboratories to evaluate methodological choices to inform shared EEG standards.

## Expected outcomes

OPES aims to develop:

1. Consensus-driven recommendations for quantitative EEG (qEEG) analysis.
2. An accompanying ontology that provides a practical reference standard.
3. A transparent, extensible framework for research and clinical neurophysiology.
4. A platform for testing reproducibility, biological validity, and harmonisation across sites in EEG studies.

## Phase 2: EEG datasets

The second phase brings together resting-state EEG recordings from healthy adults across laboratories. The datasets will be used to compare procedures at key stages of EEG processing, including:

- Automated artefact detection and channel rejection
- Re-referencing strategies
- Separation of periodic and aperiodic components of the power spectrum
- Estimation of cortical source activity and functional connectivity
- Harmonisation across electrode montages and recording systems

### We are interested in datasets with:

- Healthy adults aged 18–60
- Both sexes represented
- Participant age and educational attainment available
- Eyes-closed resting-state EEG recordings longer than one minute; eyes-open recordings are optional
- At least 64 scalp electrodes
- Sampling frequency greater than 128 Hz
- EEG recording system brand and model, reference, and ground information available

Recordings from different laboratories are welcome.

**Have a suitable dataset?** To discuss a collaboration, contact Nesli Özhan at [neslihan.ozhan@soton.ac.uk](mailto:neslihan.ozhan@soton.ac.uk).
